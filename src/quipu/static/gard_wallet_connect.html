GARD Shard wallet connect

Bind a wallet to GARD Shard marketplace access. You sign a short sign-in message. It costs nothing and authorises no transaction; no key ever leaves your wallet.

Solflare — Solana

Signs with your Solana account (ed25519).

MetaMask — Ethereum / EVM

Signs a Sign-In with Ethereum (EIP-4361) message with personal_sign.

Check that the domain in the message your wallet shows is this page's address. MetaMask warns if it is not.
