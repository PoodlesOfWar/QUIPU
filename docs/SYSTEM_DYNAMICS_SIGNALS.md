## 2. Core Control Equations

Defined in [src/quipu/learning_drive.py](../src/quipu/learning_drive.py):
$$acquisition\_drive = \text{clamp}\Big(0.20 \cdot (1-s) \cdot d + 0.10 \cdot (1-v), 0.0, 1.0\Big)$$
- $s$: Corpus saturation $[0, 1]$
- $d$: Task difficulty $[0, 1]$
- $v$: Learning velocity $[0, 1]$

Calculated in [src/quipu/observer_service.py](../src/quipu/observer_service.py):
$$EMA_{conf}(t) = 0.90 \cdot EMA_{conf}(t-1) + 0.10 \cdot c_t$$
$$suggested\_min\_confidence = \max\Big(0.35, \min\big(0.90, EMA_{conf} - 0.15\big)\Big)$$
